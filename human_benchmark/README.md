# Human benchmark

[2016200data.zip](raw/2016200data.zip) is the [journal’s replication archive](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/suppl/10.1086/705549/suppl_file/2016200data.zip) for Andreoni et al. (2020), [When Fair Isn’t Fair](https://doi.org/10.1086/705549). Ten of its 33 de-identified session CSVs cover the four lottery treatments we simulate.

`processed/` is for the benchmarks the team selects.
